# What a Word

**Speak any language, hear Polish. Instantly.**

A real-time voice interpreter that turns any spoken language into spoken and written Polish with near-zero latency. Hold the mic, speak, release — and hear yourself in Polish.

---

## Your voice, in Polish, in real time.

What a Word strips interpretation down to its essence: one button, one target language, one fluid moment between speaking and being understood. Press the mic, say your sentence in whatever language is on your tongue, and release — the Polish translation streams back as natural speech and text in the same breath. The source language is auto-detected, so there are no menus, no language pickers, no setup friction. Just one button between you and a conversation.

---

## Features

- **Real-time voice-to-voice** — Speak any language, hear fluent Polish back with minimal delay.
- **Auto-detect source language** — You don't pick a language; you just talk.
- **One-button simplicity** — Hold to speak, release to translate. Nothing to learn.
- **See it as you say it** — Your speech transcribed live on top, the Polish translation building word-by-word below.
- **Private by design** — No conversation history, no telemetry, no tracking.
- **Installable** — Add it to your home screen and launch it like a native app.

---

## How it works

1. **Sign in** — One tap.
2. **Hold & speak** — Press the mic and say it in any language.
3. **Release & hear** — Get the Polish translation as natural speech and on-screen text, instantly.

---

What a Word turns "I don't speak Polish" into a non-issue — no matter what language you do speak.
